What is Kothari Leadership?

Kothari Leadership is a growth consultancy for CEOs. We work inside a company, alongside the leader and their executive team, to unlock the growth being held back by whatever isn’t working at the top. Most CEOs describe it as the first time someone has been able to see their business clearly from the outside without also being able to hide from what’s actually happening on the inside.

The CEOs we work with have usually tried the obvious options first: a coach who’s never actually run a company, a consultant whose deliverable turned out to be a deck rather than a change, or a trusted advisor whose loyalty makes them tell you what you want to hear.

What they find in KLE is a partner who has actually done the job, who is inside their week for as long as it takes, and who will tell them the things their own team has quietly decided aren’t safe to say.

Why KLE is different

  1. We work inside the business, not from a distance.
    KLE’s coaches embed with the leadership team. They’re in the meetings, in the difficult conversations, and present while the team learns to operate at the next level. It’s the reason our clients say we get more done in 90 days than a traditional consulting engagement gets done in six months.
  2. EQ+Execution™, in the same engagement.
    Most performance problems that show up in the boardroom are actually people problems in disguise. An executive team that has quietly stopped telling the CEO what they really think. A manager who’s technically owning their department but constantly checking upward for validation. A leadership layer whose confidence hasn’t caught up with the size of the business. We work on the people side and the execution side at the same time, which is what makes either side actually shift.
  3. Straight talk. On both sides.
    KLE doesn’t soft-pedal. If we think something isn’t working, we say so. If we think a client isn’t ready, we say that too. The CEOs who’ve worked with us will consistently tell you it was the honesty they couldn’t get anywhere else that made the engagement worth the money.

What to listen for

You don’t need the exact wording. If a CEO you’re talking to sounds like any of the below, there’s a good chance KLE is worth an introduction.

  1. The CEO is still doing work only a CEO can do, plus work anyone else should be doing.

    “I know I shouldn’t be in the middle of this, but if I’m not, it doesn’t get done.” Or: “I don’t know when I last worked on the actual strategy.”

  2. The leadership team has gone quiet.

    Everyone nods in the exec meeting. Nobody argues. Then the CEO hears about the pushback later, second-hand. If they describe their leadership meetings as peaceful, that’s worth paying attention to.

  3. Managers are owning their departments but keep bringing decisions back up.

    “My VPs are good, but they keep coming to me for validation on things they should just be deciding.”

  4. They’re in the middle of something structurally difficult, or about to walk into one.

    “We just closed the deal, and now I have two ways of selling and two service models under one roof.” Or: “We’re going to double in the next two years and I don’t know if my team is ready.”

  5. They’ve tried the standard playbook and it didn’t land.

    “I had a coach for a year, nice guy, nothing really changed.” Or: “We hired a big consultancy and ended up with a beautiful deck we haven’t opened since.”

  6. The CEO is carrying more than they’re showing.

    “I don’t have anyone I can actually talk to about this. Not my board, not my team, not my family.”
